## Scoring Quirks That Change Player Value
- **6-point passing TDs** (not 4) — elevates QB value significantly
- **Big-play bonuses:** +3 points for any TD of 40+ yards (pass, rush, or receiving) — rewards deep-threat WRs and explosive backs
- **NOT PPR** — no points per reception. Pure yardage + TDs. Slot/volume receivers lose value; TD-dependent and big-play players gain.
- **Passing yards:** 0.0333 pts/yd (~33.3 yds per point)
- **Rushing/receiving yards:** 0.1 pts/yd (10 yds per point)
- **Turnovers:** -2 per INT, -2 per fumble lost
- **DST scoring:** Points against (0 PA = 9 pts), yards allowed (0-150 = 6 pts), sacks (1 pt), INTs (2 pts), fumble recoveries (2 pts), big-play TD bonuses on returns
- **What positions score most / are scarce:**
  - QBs are premium (6-pt TDs + big-play bonus). Top QBs scored 350-420 pts last season.
  - Elite RBs are scarce and dominate (McCaffrey 314, J. Taylor 325, Henry 267).
  - WRs are deep — non-PPR flattens the tier separation.
  - TE is thin as always; the gap between TE1 and TE6 is enormous.

## Roster & Lineup Rules
- **Roster size:** 18 players (min 11, max 21 including IR)
  - 2 QB, 5 RB, 5 WR, 2 TE, 2 TK (kicker), 2 DST
- **Starting lineup:** 9 starters
  - 1 QB, 1 TK, 1 DST
  - 6 flex spots across RB/WR/TE with minimums: **1 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE** required
  - Remaining 2 flex can be any combo of RB/WR/TE (max 3 RB, max 4 WR, max 2 TE)
- **IR slots:** up to 3 (any player with injury designation)
- **Lineup deadline:** 5 min before each player's gametime (not a single deadline)
- **Illegal rosters score ZERO** — critical to maintain positional limits

## Keeper & Contract Rules (the exact math)
- **Salary cap:** $200
- **Draft format:** Snake-style auction. One owner nominates, 40-second clock, $1 minimum bid increments.
- **Escalator formula:** 1.5× previous year's salary, **rounded UP to whole dollars**
  - Examples: $1 → $2 → $3 → $5 → $8 → $12 → $18 → $27 → $41 → $62
  - $2 → $3 → $5 → $8 → $12 → $18 → $27
  - $5 → $8 → $12 → $18 → $27 → $41
  - $10 → $15 → $23 → $35 → $53
- **Contract signing rules:** Players can be signed for any number of years the year AFTER they are drafted. Salary follows escalator.
- **FINAL year (option year):** After contract expires, 1-year option at **2× the final year's salary**. This is the last chance to retain before the player hits the draft pool.
- **Buyout / dead-cap formula:** Half of remaining contract salary. The full buyout cost is absorbed in the **immediately following season** (no amortization across years).
  - Example: 3-year deal at $5/$8/$12 — voiding after Year 1 costs ($4 + $6) = $10 dead cap next year.
- **Waiver-pickup keeper eligibility:** Players picked up off waivers are **NOT eligible for contracts** unless they were previously drafted or already under contract. A claimed player retains their original drafted price + escalator.
- **Keeper deadline:** August 10 each year (submitted on CBS site)

## Key Deadlines
- **Keeper deadline:** August 10
- **Trade deadline:** Before the first game of Week 10
- **Waivers open:** Week 2 (processed Wed/Fri/Sat nights)
- **Playoffs:** Weeks 15-17, top 8 teams + 8-team consolation bracket
