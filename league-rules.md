# HPFL League Rules & Settings

## League basics

- **League:** HPFL (hosted on CBS)
- **Teams:** 16
- **My team:** Xavier's Force
- **Format:** Auction draft with keeper contracts
- **Salary cap:** $200 auction budget

## Roster (18 players)

- 2 QB
- 5 RB
- 5 WR
- 2 TE
- 2 K
- 2 DST

## Starting lineup (start 9)

- 1 QB
- 6 total flex from RB/WR/TE, with minimums: 2 WR, 1 RB, 1 TE
- 1 K
- 1 DST

## Scoring

- **Rushing/receiving yards:** 0.1 pt per yard
- **Passing yards:** ~1 pt per 30 yards
- **Touchdowns:** 6 pts (all TDs)
- **Long TD bonus:** +3 for TDs of 40+ yards
- **Turnovers:** -2 per INT, -2 per fumble

## Keeper / contract system

- **Contract escalation:** each contract year costs **1.5x the previous salary, rounded up**.
- **Final option:** after a contract ends, you may keep the player **one final year at 2x their last salary**.
- **Buyouts:** cutting a player under contract costs **half the remaining salary as an immediate cap hit**.
- Uncontracted players can be released with **no buyout cost**.
- **Keeper deadline:** August 10.
